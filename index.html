FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need any technical knowledge?

Not at all. We handle the technical side from start to finish and train your team on how to use what we build — in plain English.

How long does a typical project take?

Most chatbots and automations go live in 2–4 weeks. Larger multi-step agents usually take 4–8 weeks. You'll see a working demo early in the process.

Will it work with the software we already use?

Almost certainly. We integrate with CRMs, email, calendars, spreadsheets, e-commerce platforms, help desks and most tools that have an API.

Is our data safe?

Yes. We use business-grade AI providers that don't train on your data, follow least-privilege access, and can deploy inside your own accounts so you stay in control.

What if the AI makes a mistake?

We design every system with guardrails, testing and human review where it matters. Agents escalate to a person whenever they're unsure.

Who owns what you build?

You do. Everything we build lives in your accounts and is fully documented, so you're never locked in.
