## Grammar: Typographic poster
No photos exist, so type is the imagery. Wordmark at composition scale, no
persistent nav, hard colour cuts between grounds, close inverted into small
type. Bans scrub, card rails, tilt, parallax on text.

## Signature move: la tresse
Three coloured strands hang down the right edge. Scrolling braids them: above
a braid point the strands cross over and under, below it they hang loose, and
the braid point travels down as the page advances. At the peak the braid
widens across the whole screen; at the close it is finished and tied.
